LINER NOTES

It started with this song

About ten years ago, I happened across a Project DIVA video of Senbonzakura on a music platform. At first, I was simply drawn to the character on screen and wanted to know who she was. That curiosity led me to Hatsune Miku. Looking back, that chance encounter was the beginning of these ten years.

On September 17, 2026, Senbonzakura marked fifteen years since its original upload to Niconico. The familiar melody still moves me. Its interweaving of old and new imagery also brings to mind the way tradition and modernity coexist in Japan, and the everyday lives of ordinary people through changing times.

In these unsettled times, I value all the more the simple freedom to listen to music, draw, and get to know one another in peace. I sincerely hope that young people in Japan and around the world can find more ways to connect and fewer barriers between them; that they have the courage to protect their homes and peace in the face of oppression and violence; and that they remain curious and open to new things.

Creative collaboration: あさひクロイ contributed to part of the creative process for this illustration. My sincere thanks for the thoughtful guidance.

Meeting You Is the Greatest Gift

Back in my hometown, I didn't have my usual drawing setup, so this came a little late. I finished it in a loose, sketch-like style to mark my 24th birthday and ten years since discovering Hatsune Miku.
